## 👋 About Me

I'm **Luo**, an educator and AI researcher with a Master's degree in **Computer Engineering from Hainan Normal University**.

My research explores how intelligent models can **learn from limited annotations, unpaired data, and heterogeneous sources**, and how to make them more useful in real-world applications. I enjoy connecting ideas from deep learning with practical software systems and reproducible experiments.

- 🎓 **Teaching:** AI, programming, and data-related topics
- 🔬 **Research interests:** Computer Vision, Remote Sensing, Representation Learning, Semi-supervised Learning, and Domain Adaptation
- 🌳 **Current exploration:** Cross-modal tree-crown instance segmentation and learning from unpaired observations
- 🛠️ **Engineering interests:** LLM Agents, interactive AI applications, and efficient model deployment
- 🤝 **Open to:** Research collaboration, thoughtful open-source projects, and knowledge sharing
